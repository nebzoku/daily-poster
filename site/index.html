Design a poster for the sake of it.

Daily Poster is a free design brief that lands every morning at 5.30 UK time. It takes one story from the day's news and turns it into a brief for a 9:16 poster: three headlines to choose from, the day's colour, a typeface pairing, a grid and a few photos to work with.

In a world of AI and automation, the chance to design something just because you can is getting rarer. The brief gives you just enough guardrails to explore, and nothing to overthink before you start.

Make your poster, share it with #dailyposter, and see what everyone else did with the same brief. So go, be free, play.

How it works

  1. 01

    Read the brief. One story from today's news, with everything you need underneath.

  2. 02

    Make the poster. Use the copy, colour, type and grid. Set it up in Figma in one click.

  3. 03

    Share it. Post it on Instagram with #dailyposter and the day's number tag.

The rules

  1. 01

    9:16, at 1080 × 1920 px.

  2. 02

    Use one of the headlines, or write your own from the same story in 3 to 6 words.

  3. 03

    Use the day's palette, plus black and white. No other colours.

  4. 04

    Use the typeface pairing. Size, leading, tracking and case are up to you.

  5. 05

    Build on the 6 × 12 grid.

  6. 06

    Include the date, time and source of the story.

  7. 07

    Imagery is optional. If you use a photo, credit the photographer.

[06]Grid

Margin and gutter are measured from the short side. Where things go on it is up to you.
