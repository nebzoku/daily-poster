Opera Type Machine

Daily Poster No. 036 · Sat 7 Nov · Indigo. Thousands of small letters, moved by the music: the low, mid and high sound each swell a slow wave across the page, and the pace ebbs and flows with how loud it is.

Sound

Public-domain opera: Musopen or the Internet Archive 78s. Each poster keeps its own track.

Legible headline

Calm bands where the waves settle and a headline reads, in the peak colour. They drift slowly up and down the page; loud passages wash them out, quiet ones bring them back.

Type

Animate

Move a setting back and forth between two values. Ripple makes the change roll down the page row by row, so the lines widen and tighten in a wave.

Words

One line each: headlines first, then sublines. Each poster leads with a different headline.

Colour

Two colours only uses just the background and type colours for all the type; the corner labels keep their own colour. Whole palette uses every colour in the day's palette except the background, plus mid-tones between them and a shade that fades into the background, so letters recede and come forward.
