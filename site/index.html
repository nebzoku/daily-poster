Design a poster, every day.

Daily Poster is a free design brief that lands every morning at 5.30 UK time. It takes one story from the day's news and turns it into a brief for an A-size poster: three headlines to choose from, the day's colour, a typeface pairing, a grid and a few photos to work with.

In a world of AI and automation, the chance to design something just because you can is getting rarer. The brief gives you just enough guardrails to explore, and nothing to overthink before you start.

Make your poster, share it with #dailyposter, and see what everyone else did with the same brief.

How it works

  1. 01

    Read the brief. One story from today's news, with everything you need underneath.

  2. 02

    Make the poster. Use the copy, colour, type and grid. Copy it all into Figma in one click.

  3. 03

    Share it. Post it on Instagram with #dailyposter and the day's number tag.

The rules

  1. 01

    A-size portrait, at 1080 × 1528 px.

  2. 02

    Use one of the headlines, or write your own from the same story.

  3. 03

    Use the day's palette, plus black and white. Blend modes and opacity are fair game.

  4. 04

    Use the typeface pairing. Size, leading, tracking and case are up to you.

  5. 05

    The grid is there if you want it. Use it, break it or ignore it.

  6. 06

    Include the date, time and source of the story.

  7. 07

    Imagery is optional. If you use a photo, credit the photographer.
