Every poster in the archive, cut into rectangles and moved by the music. A background to put your own type on.

Five early jazz 78s from the Internet Archive, 1921–23, so out of copyright. With no sound it idles on a slow swing.

Pattern

Each line is a line of the poster. Density sets how small the posters are, so higher density means sharper letters.

Background

Shows wherever the posters don't cover, and is what the Smear trails fade into. Texture is a soft, uneven mottle like printed paper; Noise is fine grain.

Boomerang

beats
beats

Follows the tempo of whatever is playing and finds the start of each bar (the beat counter under the poster). Every move lands exactly on a 1: the hold stretches to fill the bar. If moves land a beat early or late, tap Tap on the 1 on a downbeat or nudge a beat either way. With no sound it uses the idle swing at 96 bpm.

Any slider with ↔ on moves between its two handles: the solid one and the outlined one. Hold pauses at each end before it turns back.

Colour

Tap one colour to isolate it, then tap its neighbours to add them. The centre brings back the full wheel.

Every poster, recoloured by brightness, like a luminance mask. The first colour fills the darks, the last fills the lights. Smooth blends between them, Steps gives flat bands.

Headline

Today's headlines: tap to put one in the selected box

Every box snaps like the corner labels: the ink touches the side margins, cap tops sit on the top margin or a grid line, and the bottom baseline sits on the bottom margin. Spread fills a box from the top margin to the bottom margin with its lines evenly spaced. Use several boxes to stagger lines left and right.

Subline

Today's sublines: tap to use one, or write your own below

The paragraph sits in the top-left corner of a grid square, in the headline colour, and wraps to the width you choose in columns.

Type effects

Difference: the type inverts whatever is behind it

Texture roughens the edges like ink soaking into paper (Radius is how far, Size is how coarse). Noise is grain inside the letters. Shadow is a soft glow in the type's own colour. They apply to the headline and subline.

Corner labels

Labels sit on top, crisp, untouched by Pixelate and Blur, and are in the PNG and video.

Output

PNG saves the current frame, full bleed with no type. Recording includes the track, so you can line the cut up in After Effects.
