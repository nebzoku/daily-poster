Archive Machine

Every poster in the archive, cut into rectangles and moved by the music. A background to put your own type on.

Sound

Five early jazz 78s from the Internet Archive, 1921–23, so out of copyright. With no sound it idles on a slow swing.

Pattern

Each line is a line of the poster. Density sets how small the posters are, so higher density means sharper letters.

Colour

Tap one colour to isolate it, then tap its neighbours to add them. The centre brings back the full wheel.

Every poster, recoloured by brightness, like a luminance mask. The first colour fills the darks, the last fills the lights. Smooth blends between them, Steps gives flat bands.

Legibility

Output

PNG saves the current frame, full bleed with no type. Recording includes the track, so you can line the cut up in After Effects.
